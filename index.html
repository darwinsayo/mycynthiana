The Guide

Your first month in Cynthiana

Moving is a long list of small errands. Here's a plain checklist to start from, and we'll fill in the local details as we go.

  1. 1

    Get the lights on

    Set up electricity, water, internet, and trash pickup before moving day.

  2. 2

    Update your address

    Driver's license, vehicle registration, voter registration, and the post office.

  3. 3

    Find your people

    Church, school, clubs, and neighbors. Small towns run on introductions.

  4. 4

    Line up your helpers

    A doctor, a dentist, a vet, a mechanic, a plumber. Know who to call before you need them.

  5. 5

    Go for a walk downtown

    Coffee, lunch, and a look around. The best way to learn a town is on foot.
